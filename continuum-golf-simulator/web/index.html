How It Works

Four simple steps to transform golf simulator gameplay into an engaging, skill-based wagering experience.

1

Take Your Shot

Play on any compatible golf simulator. Place your wager and select your hole. Our system tracks your shot data in real-time.

2

Skill Assessment

Our adaptive algorithm analyzes your shot pattern using bivariate normal distribution, calculating your σ_x and σ_y (skill variance) in real-time.

3

Dynamic Payout Calculation

Your payout is determined by your shot accuracy relative to your skill level. Distance from the pin is converted to a multiplier using mathematical precision.

4

Instant Results

Receive your payout immediately with complete transparency. View your RTP, skill progression, and detailed analytics for every session.
